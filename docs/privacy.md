# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

GymLog does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All workout data is stored locally on your device.

What we collect
- Nothing. We do not collect analytics, crash reports, identifiers, or location data.

Apple Health (optional)
- If you turn on "Steps and sleep in History", GymLog reads your daily step count and time asleep from Apple Health to show them next to your training and in the CSV files you choose to export.
- This data is read on your device only. It is not stored by GymLog, not written back to Health, not sent anywhere, and not used for advertising.
- You can turn this off in Settings, or remove access in the Health app at any time.

Third-party services
- None. GymLog does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Data sharing
- None. We do not share data with third parties.

Children
- GymLog does not collect any data from users of any age.

Contact
- For questions or support, please use the Support page.
